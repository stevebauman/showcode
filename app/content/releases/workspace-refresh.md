## Floating toolbars

Language, editor settings, copy, export, and zoom now sit in compact toolbars, giving your code and preview more room.

## A collapsible inspector

Switch between Style, Themes, Backdrop, and Scenes from the bottom of the inspector. Hide it when you want more space for your canvas.

## Smoother workspace controls

Reorder editors with a smooth transition, choose your layout visually, and type a zoom percentage to get a closer look.
